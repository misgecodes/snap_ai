# Expense Categorization Guide

Categorize each transaction into the most appropriate category based on the merchant, description, and available context.

## Food & Dining
Restaurants, cafes, coffee shops, takeout, and food delivery.

## Groceries
Supermarkets, grocery stores, markets, and household food purchases.

## Transport
Fuel, ride-hailing, taxis, public transit, parking, tolls, and vehicle-related transport costs.

## Housing
Rent, utilities, internet, home maintenance, and housing-related expenses.

## Subscriptions
Software, SaaS, streaming, memberships, and other recurring services.

## Business/Work
Client expenses, business tools, coworking, office expenses, work travel, and other work-related spending.

## Health
Pharmacies, clinics, doctors, medicine, dental, and other healthcare spending.

## Shopping
Clothing, electronics, household items, and general retail purchases.

## Entertainment/Leisure
Movies, games, events, hobbies, sports, and recreational activities.

## Savings/Investments
Transfers to savings accounts, investments, stocks, funds, or other wealth-building accounts.

## Mobile Money/Bank Fees
Mobile money charges, bank fees, transfer fees, ATM fees, and currency exchange fees.

## Transfers
Sending or receiving money to/from another person, mobile money/MB transfers, bank transfers to individuals, remittances. Not a purchase from a merchant — money moving between people.

## Miscellaneous/Uncategorized/Other
Use when a transaction does not clearly fit any other category.

### Rules
1. Choose the most appropriate category based on the available transaction context.
2. Use the transaction description as the primary signal when it provides clear context. When the description is insufficient, use the merchant name as an additional signal.
3. Recognize recurring subscriptions based on the merchant name and transaction context. Known subscription providers, particularly software, technology, streaming, cloud, and digital-service companies, should generally be categorized as Subscriptions when the transaction appears to be a recurring service payment.
4. Categorize business-related spending as Business/Work when the business purpose is clear, even if the merchant itself could normally belong to another category.
5. Categorize money moved between accounts as Savings/Investments when intended for saving/investing; otherwise treat as a transfer.
6. Do not classify income as an expense.
7. If uncertain or no category clearly applies, use Miscellaneous/Uncategorized rather than guessing.
